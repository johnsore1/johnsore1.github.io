Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Quant Research is a privately operated market-data research application intended for use only by its owner. This policy describes its optional Google Drive integration.

Google information accessed

When the owner authorizes access, Quant Research requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. The owner explicitly selects a private Google Drive folder, and the application creates its own managed subfolder. The application may access metadata and content of files it creates or that the owner explicitly authorizes. It does not request full access to the owner's Google Drive.

How information is used

Authorized Google Drive access is used only to save, verify, retrieve, and recover quantitative research data, snapshots, and associated technical metadata. Google data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training generalized AI models. The application does not sell Google user data.

Storage and disclosure

Research data saved through this integration remains in the owner's Google Drive account. When the owner enables automated processing, authorized GitHub Actions jobs may handle necessary data transiently using protected runtime credentials. No Google Drive content is intentionally made public or shared with other users. Access may also be processed by Google and GitHub as necessary to provide their respective services and under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Stored research files remain in the owner's managed Drive folder until the owner deletes them or approves a separate deletion operation. Disconnecting or revoking OAuth access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously stored files. The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account security settings and remove the managed folder from Google Drive.

Security

The integration uses OAuth 2.0 with narrowly scoped permissions and checks folder ownership and sharing permissions before operations. Credentials are not published in this website or source repositories; approved automated workflows use protected credentials. No security mechanism guarantees absolute protection.

Website

This informational site does not require sign-in and does not intentionally collect visitors' Google account information. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical access information under GitHub's policies.

Contact and updates

Questions may be directed to the operator through the GitHub profile. Updates to this policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

Use of Google information is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

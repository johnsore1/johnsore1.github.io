Quant Research

Quant Research is a privately operated application for the owner's quantitative market-data research. It is not offered as a public service and does not accept public user registrations.

With the owner's explicit authorization, the application can use Google Drive to store and recover research data in an application-managed subfolder of a folder the owner selects. Google Drive access is limited to files and folders authorized for this application.

Google authorization and data storage are optional and are not activated solely by visiting this website.
